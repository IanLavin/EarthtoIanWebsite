## Getting there
- Closest airport: Calgary
- Drive time: 2 - 3 hours depending on traffic
- Trailhead / entry point: Two of the most popular locations include Marble Canyon or Stanley Glacier.

## Time & season
- Best months:  July - Sept
- Typical start time: Somewhat more flexible as the park is less heavily visited than nearby parks such as Banff.
- Permit / reservation: Parks Canada pass typically required

## What to pair it with
- Good multi-day combo: Very likely you are pairing this with neighboring Banff. Outside of national parks Radium Hot Springs and Fairmount Hot Springs lie on the other side of the park.

## Safety & prep
- Gear notes: Standard hiking gear
- Water: Bring your own
- Weather hazards: Sitting on the continental divide the weather is extremely variable, with the two canyons often experiencing dramatically different weather from one another.
