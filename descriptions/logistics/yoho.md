## Getting there
- Closest airport: Calgary
- Drive time: 2:30 - 3 hours depending on traffic
- Trailhead / entry point: The two most popular locations include Emerald Lake and Takakkaw Falls.

## Time & season
- Best months:  July - Sept
- Typical start time: Early if possible. Emerald lake looks best after 10 am with full overhead sun but parking frequently fills before 9am.
- Permit / reservation: Parks Canada pass typically required

## What to pair it with
- Good multi-day combo: Almost certainly you will be pairing this with other nearby parks such as Banff, Glacier, or Jasper.

## Safety & prep
- Gear notes: Standard hiking gear
- Water: Bring your own
- Weather hazards: The weather can be extremly variable switching from sun to clouds to rain and back within hours. Summer storms are a real concern in the afternoons.
