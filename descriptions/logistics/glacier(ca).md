## Getting there
- Closest airport: Calgary
- Drive time: 3-4 hours depending on traffic
- Trailhead / entry point: Roger pass is the most iconic area where many trails begin

## Time & season
- Best months:  July - Sept
- Typical start time: Flexible but early if hitting one of the popular desitnations like Great Glacier
- Permit / reservation: Parks Canada pass typically required

## What to pair it with
- Good multi-day combo: If time permits both Yoho National Park and Kootenay National park are each a few hours away and both equally stunning.

## Safety & prep
- Gear notes: Standard hiking gear
- Water: Bring your own
- Weather hazards: The rockies are prone to summer thunderstorms and smoke later in the summer
