- The distinct columns are actually the result of a massive volcanic eruption depositing layers of ash, which compacted and then eroded away to form the landscape seen today.
- This is one of the "sky islands" of Arizona. Known as such because the great change in elevation means that plants and animals located on the cooler upper mountain slopes cannot survive the lower hotter desert environment, making them essentially trapped on the mountain "island".
- While they are exceptionally rare, jaguars have been known to live in the area and have been sporadically spotted.
